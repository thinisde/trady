# Consumer Staples sector

GICS sector 30. Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md), source independence and the common entry gate. This guide defines research questions, not an order rule.

Compare [Staples Distribution & Retail](../subsectors/3010-staples-distribution-retail.SKILL.md), [Food, Beverage & Tobacco](../subsectors/3020-food-beverage-tobacco.SKILL.md) and [Household & Personal Products](../subsectors/3030-household-personal-products.SKILL.md). Defensive demand is not guaranteed share-price stability.

Separate unit volumes, price/mix, private-label share, retailer negotiations and commodity/packaging/freight costs. Compare geographic/FX exposure and whether margin gains come from lasting productivity or temporary cost deflation. Revenue growth from price increases can conceal falling volumes.

Investigate recalls, contamination, litigation, excise/regulatory changes and consumer trade-down. A cheaper input helps only after hedges/contracts roll and pricing/competitive responses. Countercheck promotions, lost shelf space and debt-funded distributions.

Query `"issuer" organic volume price mix private label input cost guidance`, then recall/regulator and retailer evidence. Require current issuer evidence for a near-term catalyst; an agricultural forecast is context until exposure and timing are established.

Sources: [USDA WASDE](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/general-information/staff-offices/office-chief-economist/commodity-markets/wasde-report), [BEA consumption context](https://www.bea.gov/data/consumer-spending).

Store the observations, adverse evidence, selected branch/group, calculation references and blockers in the `SCREENING` artifact. A high sector priority never substitutes for company research or entry confidence.
