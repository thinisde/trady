# Financials sector

GICS sector 40. Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md), source independence and the common entry gate. This guide defines research questions, not an order rule.

Compare [Banks](../subsectors/4010-banks.SKILL.md), [Financial Services](../subsectors/4020-financial-services.SKILL.md) and [Insurance](../subsectors/4030-insurance.SKILL.md). Higher rates can increase asset yields while worsening funding, credit, duration losses or asset-management flows.

Identify the business model, balance-sheet exposure and relevant regulator. Check credit/deposit quality, capital/liquidity, duration and fee/underwriting drivers. Distinguish recurring operating earnings from trading gains, provision releases, reserve changes and asset disposals.

Read issuer filings alongside aggregate FDIC/Fed or insurance data; aggregate reports lag and do not verify today's rumor about one institution. Countercheck concentration, uninsured funding, collateral, counterparty risk and regulatory restrictions. A bank with an expanding margin but weakening asset quality is a mixed case.

Query `"issuer" capital liquidity funding credit loss guidance` plus the appropriate group metrics. Resolve any material solvency/funding contradiction before entry; high yields or a low multiple alone do not resolve it.

Sources: [FDIC banking profile](https://www.fdic.gov/quarterly-banking-profile), [Fed data](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data.htm), [NAIC industry reports](https://content.naic.org/industry/insurance-industry-snapshots-analysis-reports).

Store the observations, adverse evidence, selected branch/group, calculation references and blockers in the `SCREENING` artifact. A high sector priority never substitutes for company research or entry confidence.
