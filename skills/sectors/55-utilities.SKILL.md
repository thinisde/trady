# Utilities sector

GICS sector 55. Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md), source independence and the common entry gate. This guide defines research questions, not an order rule.

Utilities has one [industry-group guide](../subsectors/5510-utilities.SKILL.md); narrow further to regulated electric/gas/water, merchant generation or renewable operators and their geography. A large power-demand headline need not improve the regulated shareholder's near-term return.

Check load growth, contracted versus merchant pricing, fuel mix, allowed returns, rate-case status, capital program/funding and leverage. Ask who pays for new capacity and when costs enter rates. Compare weather-normalized demand with a short-lived temperature spike.

Look for outages, wildfire/storm liability, fuel hedges, interconnection limits and regulatory disallowance. Countercheck dilution/refinancing, customer affordability and announced data-center load with no executed connection agreement. Nuclear/renewable capacity announcements need permitting and construction context.

Query `"issuer" rate case allowed return capex load guidance` and the actual regulator/customer documents. Demand can be strategically interesting without a fresh tradable catalyst; do not treat the sector as risk-free bond exposure.

Sources: [EIA electricity](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/), [FERC reports](https://www.ferc.gov/staff-reports-and-papers).

Store the observations, adverse evidence, selected branch/group, calculation references and blockers in the `SCREENING` artifact. A high sector priority never substitutes for company research or entry confidence.
