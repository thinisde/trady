# Information Technology sector

GICS sector 45. Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md), source independence and the common entry gate. This guide defines research questions, not an order rule.

Compare [Software & Services](../subsectors/4510-software-services.SKILL.md), [Hardware & Equipment](../subsectors/4520-technology-hardware-equipment.SKILL.md) and [Semiconductors](../subsectors/4530-semiconductors-equipment.SKILL.md). AI/search/media themes also involve issuers classified in Communication Services; preserve actual GICS mapping.

Identify the payer, monetization, production/compute capacity, competitive substitute and incremental margin. Read customer/supplier guidance, not only a vendor demo. Bookings, backlog, usage, annual recurring revenue and recognized revenue are not interchangeable.

Check export/geographic restrictions, concentration, infrastructure capex/energy needs and cash conversion. Countercheck cannibalization, price compression, channel inventory, capacity constraints, security incidents and delayed customer deployment. Do not assume all suppliers receive the same margin from a hyperscaler spending announcement.

Query `"issuer" "product" customer revenue margin capacity guidance` plus cancellation/restriction/competition queries. A hypothetical invention needs evidence of commercial stage and materiality before it can support any higher target.

Sources: [SEC original disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), [BIS restriction context](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744).

Store the observations, adverse evidence, selected branch/group, calculation references and blockers in the `SCREENING` artifact. A high sector priority never substitutes for company research or entry confidence.
