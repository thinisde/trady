# Real Estate sector

GICS sector 60. Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md), source independence and the common entry gate. This guide defines research questions, not an order rule.

Compare [Equity REITs](../subsectors/6010-equity-reits.SKILL.md) with [Real Estate Management & Development](../subsectors/6020-real-estate-management-development.SKILL.md). Mortgage REITs are not automatically equity REITs; follow their actual effective classification and supported universe.

Choose the property/customer branch: data center, logistics, residential, retail, office, health-related or other verified exposure. Check occupancy/rent spreads, tenant concentration, financing maturities, interest coverage, development obligations and capital costs. A rate decline can help financing yet coincide with weaker tenant demand.

Read issuer reconciliations for FFO/AFFO and cash measures; definitions can differ. Countercheck concessions, tenant defaults, refinancing/dilution, cap-rate changes and unfunded projects. A high dividend does not establish a total-return opportunity.

Query `"issuer" same store NOI occupancy lease spread debt maturity guidance` and tenant/development counterevidence. Distinguish a signed lease from occupied rent-paying capacity and announced pipeline from funded delivery.

Sources: [Nareit FFO guidance](https://www.reit.com/nareit/advocacy/policy/nareit-ffo-white-paper-and-related-implementation), [Census property activity](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/).

Store the observations, adverse evidence, selected branch/group, calculation references and blockers in the `SCREENING` artifact. A high sector priority never substitutes for company research or entry confidence.
