# Health Care sector

GICS sector 35. Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md), source independence and the common entry gate. This guide defines research questions, not an order rule.

Choose between [Equipment & Services](../subsectors/3510-health-care-equipment-services.SKILL.md) and [Pharma, Biotech & Life Sciences](../subsectors/3520-pharma-biotech-life-sciences.SKILL.md). Higher procedure utilization can help a provider/device firm while increasing a payer's costs.

Map the actual product, patient population, stage, payer and economic owner. Clinical activity, successful endpoints, clearance/approval, coverage/payment and commercial sales are separate milestones. Product novelty cannot replace study quality, safety and financial materiality.

Check registered endpoints/comparator and complete safety/results where relevant, plus effective reimbursement/rule dates, staffing costs and management guidance. Countercheck competing products, patent exposure, recalls, reimbursement cuts and trial/manufacturing delays. A proposed rule is not final law or revenue.

Query `"issuer" "product" approval endpoint safety reimbursement guidance` and the original regulator/trial record. Never convert a press-release superlative into an exceptional target. Unread material primary evidence or unresolved stage ambiguity blocks entry.

Sources: [FDA development stages](https://www.fda.gov/patients/learn-about-drug-and-device-approvals/drug-development-process), [CMS expenditure context](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/statistics-trends-and-reports/national-health-expenditure-data).

Store the observations, adverse evidence, selected branch/group, calculation references and blockers in the `SCREENING` artifact. A high sector priority never substitutes for company research or entry confidence.
