# Communication Services sector

GICS sector 50. Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md), source independence and the common entry gate. This guide defines research questions, not an order rule.

Separate [Telecommunication Services](../subsectors/5010-telecommunication-services.SKILL.md) from [Media & Entertainment](../subsectors/5020-media-entertainment.SKILL.md). A broadband capex story, a content hit and AI-driven advertising economics have different beneficiaries.

Read subscriber/engagement definitions, pricing, churn, ad pricing/impressions, content/network costs and free cash flow. Distinguish customers gained by promotions from profitable retention, and reported users from paying subscribers. Match segment and geography.

Investigate competition, platform policy, spectrum, privacy/antitrust, distribution contracts and network reliability. Countercheck rising acquisition costs, content amortization/rights costs, capex and lower ad demand. Popular engagement or CEO praise does not prove monetization.

Query `"issuer" subscribers churn ARPU advertising margin guidance` plus FCC/regulator/competitor and disputed-partnership evidence. Require primary financial/operating evidence and independent comparison before interpreting a viral product story as an entry.

Sources: [FCC reports](https://www.fcc.gov/reports-research), [SEC original disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

Store the observations, adverse evidence, selected branch/group, calculation references and blockers in the `SCREENING` artifact. A high sector priority never substitutes for company research or entry confidence.
