# Memory and review

## Retrieve balanced context

Read the relevant session summary, instrument/event knowledge, accepted/rejected plans and actual computed outcomes. Include comparable losing and no-action examples when available, not only profitable trades. Keep timestamps, evidence/plan IDs, trust class and contradictions attached to remembered claims.

A memory note is not a policy rule or financial record. Reconcile old conclusions with newer primary corrections and current positions. Do not revive deleted/expired source text through a copied summary, quote or retrieved lesson.

## Summarize for continuation

Capture the current question/thesis, established facts and citations, unresolved questions, important tool results and gaps, accepted/rejected updates and next useful work. Preserve distinctions between a requested action, a proposed plan and a broker-confirmed outcome.

Do not turn a simplified summary into the authoritative account balance, held quantity, order status or active threshold. The next research run refreshes those facts from application views. Keep unresolved call/result pairs intact; session compaction must not fabricate completed tools.

## Review outcomes

Retain original model predictions and their availability/production times,
target/horizon/scenario and calibration revisions. Score only matured outcomes;
pending labels remain pending. Record cumulative errors, sample counts, drift,
regime gaps and repeated evidence without reconstructing historical forecasts
with today's LLM. The [offline weekly Codex workflow](../../docs/v1/weekly-optimization.md)
is separate from this runtime skill and cannot receive broker/deployment authority.

Use `portfolio.performance` and reconciled records for numbers. Distinguish deposits from gains, realized from unrealized results, brokerage costs from outside operating expenses and filled round trips from pending orders. A profitable holding without a sale is not a completed win.

Identify specific process observations: evidence was copied, a source correction arrived late, costs eliminated the setup, a confidence component was unsupported, or a control prevented a duplicate/late action. State sample size, counterexamples and uncertainty. Do not infer a reliable strategy from a few wins.

Propose an attributed lesson or testable improvement rather than automatically changing risk limits, scores, target ceilings, source permissions, tools or this skill bundle. The application may store approved derived summaries and memory projections under its retention rules; you do not choose filesystem paths or write arbitrary files.

## Result

Return the bounded summary/review in final JSON with references and proposed lessons in structured research findings. Follow [memory and data](../../docs/v1/memory-and-data.md), [performance reporting](../../docs/v1/operations.md#performance-and-records) and the [JSON protocol](../../docs/v1/llm-and-tools.md).
