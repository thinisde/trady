# Commercial & Professional Services subsector

GICS industry group 2020; parent [sector guide](../sectors/20-industrials.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Separate facilities/environmental/security services, staffing, consulting and research/professional services. For project labor, inspect billable utilization, bill rates, headcount and compensation. For outsourced recurring services, check renewal/retention, contract pricing, route/customer density, pass-through terms and input costs. For staffing, distinguish temporary placements and permanent hiring; recession sensitivity differs.

Compare organic growth with acquisitions, recurring with one-off contract wins, and signed contract value with funded service demand. A large multiyear award can contribute little near-term profit. Check wage inflation and whether repricing catches up before calling it margin expansion.

Query `"issuer" utilization bill rate wage renewal organic guidance` and `"customer" outsourcing contract termination`. Countercheck slower client hiring/spend, public procurement delays, safety/liability issues, weak collections and customer concentration. Compare peers with the same labor and contract model.

Personalize monitoring to major renewals, client demand and labor costs. Sources: [Census services indicators](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/), [SEC issuer disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
