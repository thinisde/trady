# Banks subsector

GICS industry group 4010; parent [sector guide](../sectors/40-financials.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Compare net interest margin with asset yields, deposit mix/beta, wholesale funding and loan demand. Inspect charge-offs, delinquency/nonperforming loans, provisions/coverage, commercial real-estate concentration, unrealized duration exposure, capital and usable liquidity. A provision release can boost earnings without improving credit.

Read issuer filings/call reports and current management disclosures; FDIC aggregates are lagged sector context. Do not use an aggregate liquidity ratio to verify a specific bank's solvency rumor. Distinguish bank subsidiary from listed holding company and currency/jurisdiction.

Query `"issuer" deposits funding net interest margin charge offs capital` and `"issuer" commercial real estate liquidity regulator`. Countercheck deposit outflows, expensive promotional funding, concentrated borrowers, collateral and regulatory distribution limits. Compare peers by lending/funding mix, not headline price/book alone.

Watch official results, deposit/funding changes, credit and regulator actions. An unverified bank-run post is attributed rumor needing investigation, not an automatic exit command. Sources: [FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile](https://www.fdic.gov/quarterly-banking-profile), [Fed SLOOS](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/sloos.htm).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
