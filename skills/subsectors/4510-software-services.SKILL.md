# Software & Services subsector

GICS industry group 4510; parent [sector guide](../sectors/45-information-technology.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Separate subscription software, infrastructure/cloud, IT consulting and other services. Inspect recurring revenue/retention definitions, customer additions/churn, bookings/remaining obligations, recognized growth, gross margin, sales efficiency, cash flow and stock-based dilution. For consulting, compare utilization, bookings and hiring/subcontract costs; for cloud, demand, capacity/capex and power constraints.

A new AI feature, benchmark or usage spike is not paid adoption or profitable incremental revenue. Check whether it replaces existing license revenue, adds compute costs or depends on an unsettled partner arrangement. Customer/partner original statements are crucial.

Query `"issuer" retention bookings remaining obligations AI monetization margin capex`, then `"product" churn price cut security outage migration`. Countercheck cannibalization, weak renewals, free promotion, high compute costs, customer concentration and changed KPI definitions. Compare peers with similar recurring/consumption economics.

Watch customer commitments, earnings guidance, service incidents and disclosed relationships. Sources: [SEC disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), actual issuer/customer earnings and product disclosures.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
