# Technology Hardware & Equipment subsector

GICS industry group 4520; parent [sector guide](../sectors/45-information-technology.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose devices/servers, network equipment or electronic components/manufacturing. Compare unit shipments, average selling price, installed/replacement cycle, backlog, channel inventory, bill of materials, warranty/returns and geographic/customer mix. For contract manufacturing, inspect utilization, customer concentration and working capital; for network equipment, funded customer capex and deployment schedules.

A server order, design win or product announcement can be conditional and far ahead of recognized revenue. Separate component pass-through revenue from value-added margin, and organic demand from acquisition/FX changes.

Query `"issuer" units ASP backlog channel inventory margin customer`, then `"product" delay cancellation export recall`. Countercheck double ordering, promotional pricing, weak end-market sell-through, supplier bottlenecks, tariffs/restrictions and currency. Compare similar customer/cycle exposure rather than all hardware.

Watch the actual OEM/customer/contract manufacturer chain and product milestones. Sources: [Census manufacturing context](https://www.census.gov/manufacturing/m3/), [SEC issuer filings](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), relevant current BIS decisions.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
