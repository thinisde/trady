# Financial Services subsector

GICS industry group 4020; parent [sector guide](../sectors/40-financials.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose the actual model: payment networks/processors, exchanges/brokers, asset managers, diversified/consumer finance, mortgage REITs or other classified services. For payments, compare volume, transactions, cross-border mix and take rate. For managers, separate net flows from market-driven assets under management and fee yield. For brokers/exchanges, inspect volumes, activity/clearing, fee mix and regulation.

For consumer finance, inspect credit losses, funding, receivables and customer health. For mortgage REITs, inspect financing, leverage, duration/hedges and book-value sensitivity; do not apply an equity-property REIT occupancy thesis. Company-defined net/adjusted measures require reconciliation.

Query `"issuer" volume take rate net flows credit loss funding guidance` with branch-specific competitors/regulator evidence. Countercheck fee compression, fraud/credit losses, clearing/counterparty exposure, weak flows and legal changes. More trading volatility need not improve every intermediary's risk-adjusted earnings.

Watch actual transaction/customer/credit or rate exposure. Sources: [SEC filings](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), [Federal Reserve data](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data.htm).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
