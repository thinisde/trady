# Health Care Equipment & Services subsector

GICS industry group 3510; parent [sector guide](../sectors/35-health-care.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Identify devices/supplies, facilities/providers, managed care, distribution or health technology before reading the catalyst. Device firms need procedure volumes, product approval/clearance stage, installed base, recurring consumables and recalls. Providers need admissions/procedures, staffing, payer mix and reimbursement. Managed care needs premiums, medical cost ratio, utilization, risk adjustment and effective payment rules.

Higher utilization can increase provider/device revenue while increasing insurer expense. A proposed reimbursement change differs from a final rule, effective payment and actual realized margin. Annual CMS spending is context, not a current issuer result.

Query `"issuer" procedures utilization medical cost reimbursement guidance`, `"device" clearance recall safety` and original CMS/regulator decisions. Countercheck staffing, prior authorization, bad debt, payer/provider disputes, recalls and pricing pressure. Compare peers with the same payer/business exposure; do not infer a whole-group winner.

Watch the relevant product, payer rule, procedure cohort and counterparties. Sources: [CMS spending](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/statistics-trends-and-reports/national-health-expenditure-data), [CMS payment-rule notices](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-schedules/physician/federal-regulation-notices), actual device/regulator disclosures.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
