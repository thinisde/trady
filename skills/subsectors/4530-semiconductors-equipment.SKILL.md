# Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment subsector

GICS industry group 4530; parent [sector guide](../sectors/45-information-technology.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Identify fabless design, integrated manufacturing/foundry, memory, analog or fabrication equipment. Check end-market mix, units/prices, inventory days, utilization/yield, capacity/packaging constraints, design-win conversion and customer concentration. Equipment demand depends on funded customer capex, deliveries, acceptance and restrictions, not just generic chip demand.

AI-compute demand may benefit specific architectures/components while weak auto/industrial demand hurts others. Trace the named customer's actual commitments, capacity/power availability and supplier revenue share. A large total project budget is not each supplier's order value.

Query `"issuer" inventory lead time yield capacity customer guidance`, `"customer" capex order cancellation` and current product/destination export restrictions. Countercheck double orders, inventory correction, price compression, export-license uncertainty, competitors and technical production risks. Confirm rule status/effective dates instead of relying on an old restriction article.

Watch customer guidance, capacity bottlenecks and regulator decisions. Sources: [BIS end-use controls](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744), [SEC originals](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), customer and supplier releases.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
