# Consumer Discretionary Distribution & Retail subsector

GICS industry group 2550; parent [sector guide](../sectors/25-consumer-discretionary.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose online, broadline, specialty or distribution exposure. Compare comparable sales with traffic and ticket, merchandise/category mix, inventory age, markdowns, returns, freight/fulfillment, shrink and advertising/member-service contribution. Store openings, acquisitions and currency must be separated from comparable demand.

For platforms, distinguish gross merchandise value from net revenue/take rate and third-party versus owned inventory. For distributors, inspect working capital, customer credit and price pass-through. Report company-defined KPI changes before comparing periods.

Query `"issuer" comparable traffic ticket inventory markdown fulfillment guidance` and `"issuer" returns consumer weakness cancellation`. Countercheck discount-driven sales, expensive delivery subsidies, supplier concentration and tariff timing. A sales headline without gross-margin/cash evidence stays incomplete.

Watch material category suppliers, seasonal periods and execution costs; do not follow every consumer trend. Sources: [Census retail indicators](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/), [BEA real/nominal spending context](https://www.bea.gov/news/pio-release-additional-information).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
