# Media & Entertainment subsector

GICS industry group 5020; parent [sector guide](../sectors/50-communication-services.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose interactive platforms/search, broadcasting/cable/content, streaming or games. For advertising, inspect impressions, price, advertiser demand and acquisition/distribution costs. For streaming, compare paying subscribers, churn, ARPU, ad tiers, content costs and profit/cash flow. For games/media, check actual release/rights economics, bookings versus recognized revenue and recurring engagement.

AI features may defend or cannibalize existing search/advertising economics. A viral title or user metric needs a payer, sustained retention and margin channel. Read original executive remarks, actual distribution/rights contracts and counterparty statements.

Query `"issuer" advertising price impressions subscribers ARPU content margin` and `"product" churn competition rights antitrust`. Countercheck expensive content/customer acquisition, delayed releases, platform policy, lower ad yields, privacy/antitrust and fake engagement. Compare peers with the same monetization model.

Watch actual product/earnings, advertisers/distributors and verified critical partners. Sources: [FCC research](https://www.fcc.gov/reports-research), [SEC financial disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), original company transcripts.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
