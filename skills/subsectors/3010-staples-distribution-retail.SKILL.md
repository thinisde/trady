# Consumer Staples Distribution & Retail subsector

GICS industry group 3010; parent [sector guide](../sectors/30-consumer-staples.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Check food/staples unit demand, comparable transactions/ticket, price inflation, private-label mix and merchandise margin. For membership businesses, inspect renewals, fee changes and paid members separately from store-sales growth. For distributors, compare delivered volume, customer mix, route productivity and supplier pass-through.

Staples demand can be resilient while margins suffer from wages, shrink, logistics or price competition. Separate a lower selling-price environment from falling unit demand. Grocery inflation does not automatically create real growth.

Query `"issuer" transaction volume inflation private label renewal shrink margin` and supplier/customer counterevidence. Countercheck trade-down into cheaper channels, labor/food costs, lost customers, food-safety recalls and price-investment campaigns. Compare same-model retailers rather than a club's fee economics with an ordinary grocer.

Watch major supplier/product recalls, renewal/fee and operating-cost events. Sources: [Census retail indicators](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/), [USDA food-input context](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/general-information/staff-offices/office-chief-economist/commodity-markets/wasde-report).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
