# Consumer Services subsector

GICS industry group 2530; parent [sector guide](../sectors/25-consumer-discretionary.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

For hotels/travel/leisure, compare bookings with cancellations, occupancy, average rates, revenue per available room and cost/capacity. For restaurants, separate transactions, average ticket and new-unit growth; track food/labor, franchise versus owned mix and promotional spending. For gaming/other services, use the actual regulatory/customer model, same-property activity and acquisition/retention costs.

Check whether demand is domestic/international, premium/value and repeatable or event-driven. Advance bookings and sold-out events do not equal recognized profit. A franchise's accounting differs from a company-owned operator.

Query `"issuer" transactions occupancy rate booking cancellation margin guidance`, then labor/food cost, customer weakness and relevant local regulation. Countercheck discount-driven traffic, labor shortages, one-off calendar effects, construction costs and customer acquisition spend. Compare peers with the same operating/ownership model.

Watch the selected venue/customer cohort and promised milestone. Sources: [BEA service spending](https://www.bea.gov/data/consumer-spending), [Census service/retail indicators](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/), original issuer operating reports.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
