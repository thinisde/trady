# Materials subsector

GICS industry group 1510; parent [sector guide](../sectors/15-materials.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

For chemicals, compare product/feedstock spreads, volumes, operating rates and capacity; for metals/mining, product mix, realized prices, grade/recovery, energy costs and mine availability. For construction materials, compare regional volumes/pricing and infrastructure/housing demand. For packaging/paper, inspect shipment volumes, recycling/fiber/energy costs, contract pass-through and capacity closures/additions.

A commodity shortage benefits only issuers with saleable output at favorable margins; a new resource, permit or capacity plan may take years. Match customers and geography, and compare same-product peers. Slow USGS baselines inform supply exposure, not a minute-by-minute price signal.

Query `"issuer" "material" volume price cost utilization` and `"facility" capacity delay permit`. Countercheck weak end-market orders, cheaper substitutes, inventory accumulation, environmental obligations and hedged/contracted pricing. Reject a thesis that equates higher revenue from inflation with improving profitability without cost evidence.

The watchdog follows the selected material, facility, input and customer end market. Sources: [USGS commodity data](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/commodity-statistics-and-information), [Census manufacturing](https://www.census.gov/manufacturing/m3/).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
