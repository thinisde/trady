# Household & Personal Products subsector

GICS industry group 3030; parent [sector guide](../sectors/30-consumer-staples.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Track organic unit volume, price/mix, market/channel share, premium/value trade-down, innovation launch costs and geographic/FX exposure. Separate recurring productivity from temporary raw-material savings. Resins, pulp, chemicals, packaging and promotion costs have different timing and pass-through.

Ask whether a launch is available, winning repeat purchases and improving profit rather than only receiving favorable influencer attention. Retailer commentary can reveal shelf-space, inventory and private-label pressure that a supplier's release omits.

Query `"issuer" organic volume price mix market share input cost` and `"brand" retailer recall lawsuit promotion`. Countercheck weak units hidden by pricing, expensive acquisition/marketing, distribution losses, safety/quality issues and regulatory product claims. Compare household essentials separately from discretionary beauty exposure within the actual business mix.

Watch material retailer, brand-safety and input changes. A social endorsement is an attributed mention until supported by operating evidence. Sources: [BEA consumption context](https://www.bea.gov/data/consumer-spending), [SEC issuer facts](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
