# Automobiles & Components subsector

GICS industry group 2510; parent [sector guide](../sectors/25-consumer-discretionary.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Separate vehicle producers, parts/components and tires. For automakers, inspect deliveries versus production, inventory, incentives/price cuts, finance affordability, product/regional mix and automotive margin. Separate recurring automotive profit from credits and unusual items. For suppliers, check customer production schedules, content per vehicle, contract repricing and warranty exposure.

An unveiling, preorder/reservation, regulatory clearance, factory start and customer delivery are different milestones. A CEO's statement about autonomous products requires the actual capability/regulatory/commercial stage, not a self-assigned breakthrough label.

Query `"issuer" deliveries production inventory incentive automotive margin`, `"product" recall approval availability`, and the component supplier/customer names. Countercheck demand pulled forward by subsidies, tariffs, financing, price competition, quality recalls and launch delays. Compare like vehicle/geographic mix.

Follow actual delivery/guidance releases and the relevant regulator/customer chain. Sources: [Census retail/manufacturing indicators](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/), [SEC filings](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
