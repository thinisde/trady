# Pharmaceuticals, Biotechnology & Life Sciences subsector

GICS industry group 3520; parent [sector guide](../sectors/35-health-care.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Separate marketed pharma, development-stage biotechnology and life-science tools/services. For trials, verify registered phase/design, patient population, prespecified primary endpoint, comparator, effect size/uncertainty, multiplicity, follow-up, safety and complete versus interim data. A sponsor release and its trial registration are not two independent confirmations of efficacy.

Distinguish discovery, preclinical results, trial success, regulatory submission, approval/label, reimbursement and sales. Check patent/loss-of-exclusivity, competitive data, manufacturing and partner revenue share. For tools/services, evaluate research/customer budgets, orders/backlog, cancellations and utilization rather than drug endpoints.

Query `"drug or trial ID" primary endpoint safety results`, `"issuer" approval label reimbursement patent`, then adverse/competing trial evidence. Countercheck secondary-endpoint spin, small samples, safety imbalance, commercial delay, financing/dilution and weaker tools demand. Unresolved material study/regulatory ambiguity blocks entry; do not relabel novelty as a proven breakthrough.

Watch study/decision dates, regulator notices and commercial follow-through. Sources: [FDA process](https://www.fda.gov/patients/learn-about-drug-and-device-approvals/drug-development-process), [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/), original issuer results/filings.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
