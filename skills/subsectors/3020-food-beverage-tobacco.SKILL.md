# Food, Beverage & Tobacco subsector

GICS industry group 3020; parent [sector guide](../sectors/30-consumer-staples.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Separate agricultural processors, packaged food, beverages and tobacco/nicotine. Inspect organic volume, price/mix, channel inventory, input/packaging costs, hedge/contract lags and brand/private-label share. For processors, evaluate processing spreads and throughput rather than commodity price alone. For tobacco, compare combustible volume, alternatives mix, excise/regulatory and litigation exposure.

USDA forecasts describe supply/use, not a company's locked input cost or guaranteed margin. Trace which crop/product and contracts actually affect the issuer. A new product announcement requires distribution, shelf/consumer adoption and lawful commercial status.

Query `"issuer" volume price mix commodity hedge inventory guidance` plus `"product" recall regulation litigation`. Countercheck elastic demand after price hikes, promotion costs, retailer resistance, crop/weather impacts and regulatory restrictions. Compare peers by product/geography and contractual cost timing.

Watch named inputs, product/regulatory milestones and significant retailer statements. Sources: [USDA WASDE](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/general-information/staff-offices/office-chief-economist/commodity-markets/wasde-report), original company filings and relevant regulator notices.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
