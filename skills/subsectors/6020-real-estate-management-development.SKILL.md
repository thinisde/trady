# Real Estate Management & Development subsector

GICS industry group 6020; parent [sector guide](../sectors/60-real-estate.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Separate real-estate services/management, transaction brokerage and development/operations. For fee services, inspect recurring contracts, assets/buildings under management, outsourcing renewals and margins. For brokerage, compare transaction volumes, financing availability, fee yield and property/geographic mix. For developers, inspect presales/contracts, completion schedule, land/inventory carrying costs and funding.

A transaction recovery can help brokerage fees without improving owned-property values or development cash flow. Announced development value and recurring fees have different risk/timing. Do not use equity-REIT FFO as the universal metric for these businesses.

Query `"issuer" leasing transaction fee recurring management development cash` and `"project" finance cancellation delay valuation`. Countercheck weak capital markets, cancelled transactions, client concentration, leverage/guarantees, project overruns and uncollected fees. Compare like business models and separate organic growth from acquisitions.

Watch major client mandates, financing and actual transaction/project completions. An empty eligible-member set for a branch is a valid result. Sources: [Census property/construction indicators](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/), [SEC issuer disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
