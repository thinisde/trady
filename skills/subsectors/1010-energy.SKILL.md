# Energy subsector

GICS industry group 1010; parent [sector guide](../sectors/10-energy.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose a branch before selecting a stock. For upstream, inspect realized oil/gas prices, production volumes, hedge coverage, lifting costs, decline rates and capital/return policy. For integrated/refining firms, compare crude inputs with product prices, utilization/outages and chemical margins. For pipelines/storage, check contracted volumes, tariff terms, counterparty quality and debt; for equipment/services, orders, customer budgets and backlog conversion.

Cross-check EIA inventories with product supplied, imports/exports and seasonal/revision effects; a crude draw alone does not prove demand acceleration. Compare peers with similar product and geography, not an oil producer against a fee-based gas transporter.

Query `"issuer" realized price hedge production capex`, `"refinery" outage utilization margin` or `"issuer" contracted volume backlog cancellation` according to branch. Recheck adverse facts: demand destruction, weaker crack spreads, pipeline/customer disruption and service pricing pressure. A commodity move without material issuer exposure is not enough.

Watch the relevant release, facility/region and customer statement, not every energy headline. Sources: [EIA petroleum report](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/), [EIA gas report](https://www.eia.gov/naturalgas/weekly/).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
