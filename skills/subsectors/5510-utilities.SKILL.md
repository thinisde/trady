# Utilities subsector

GICS industry group 5510; parent [sector guide](../sectors/55-utilities.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Narrow the single industry group to regulated electric/gas/water, multi-utility, merchant generation or renewable operators. For regulated businesses, inspect allowed return, rate base, approved versus proposed rate cases, cost recovery, funding and service territory. For merchant generation, use contracted/spot power, fuel spreads, hedge coverage and plant availability.

Data-center demand requires actual connection/offtake terms, available infrastructure and who finances expansion. Weather-driven consumption is not structural demand. Compare fuel/technology, territory, regulatory framework and financing; avoid a uniform rates-down thesis.

Query `"issuer" rate case cost recovery load connection capex financing` or `"plant" availability hedge fuel margin`. Countercheck disallowances, delays, debt/equity issuance, wildfire/storm liability, outage, environmental obligations and demand weaker than proposed projects imply.

Watch named rate proceedings, major customers, plants and reliability/fuel conditions. Sources: [EIA Electricity Monthly](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/), [FERC market/reliability reports](https://www.ferc.gov/staff-reports-and-papers), original state-regulator and issuer records.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
