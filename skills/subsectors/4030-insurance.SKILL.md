# Insurance subsector

GICS industry group 4030; parent [sector guide](../sectors/40-financials.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose property/casualty, life/annuity, reinsurance or brokerage. For P&C/reinsurance, compare combined ratio including/excluding catastrophe and prior-year reserve development, premium rate versus exposure growth, retention and reinsurance cost. For life/annuities, inspect spreads, duration, surrender behavior, guarantees and capital. For brokerage, use organic fees/commissions and retention rather than an underwriting ratio.

Investment gains, favorable reserves or one mild-catastrophe period may mask weak underlying economics. Gross versus net losses and statutory versus GAAP capital are different measures. Compare like geographies, lines and reporting bases.

Query `"issuer" combined ratio reserve development catastrophe pricing capital` or `"issuer" annuity spread surrender hedge`. Countercheck social inflation, reserve inadequacy, event losses, counterparties, regulatory limits and distribution costs. A disaster headline needs verified exposure/coverage, not an assumption that every insurer loses equally.

Watch line-specific events, renewals, reserve/capital disclosures and claims updates. Sources: [NAIC industry reports](https://content.naic.org/industry/insurance-industry-snapshots-analysis-reports), original insurer filings and statutory disclosures.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
