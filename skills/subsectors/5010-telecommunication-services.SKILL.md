# Telecommunication Services subsector

GICS industry group 5010; parent [sector guide](../sectors/50-communication-services.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Separate wireless, fixed/broadband, integrated and relevant alternative network operators. Compare net additions with churn, average revenue per user/account definitions, acquisition/promotional cost, equipment subsidies, service margin, capex, spectrum commitments and free cash flow. Network reach is not paying-customer adoption.

Check competition by geography/service, fiber or wireless substitution and whether price increases improve value after churn. A spectrum auction, coverage announcement or network partnership needs its financing, terms and deployment timeline.

Query `"issuer" service revenue churn ARPU capex spectrum guidance` and `"market" broadband competition outage regulator`. Countercheck heavy debt/refinancing, promotions, quality outages, litigation and unprofitable additions. Compare similar metrics and accounting; ARPU per subscriber and per account are different denominators.

Watch material network incidents, regulator/spectrum and actual customer-price changes. Personal CEO commentary requires company/regulator corroboration for material claims. Sources: [FCC reports](https://www.fcc.gov/reports-research), [SEC issuer filings](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
