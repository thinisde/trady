# Capital Goods subsector

GICS industry group 2010; parent [sector guide](../sectors/20-industrials.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Determine whether the issuer sells long-cycle equipment, construction services, defense systems or aftermarket parts. Read orders/book-to-bill definitions, funded backlog, cancellation clauses, delivery slots, price escalation, fixed-price cost exposure and cash/working-capital conversion. A backlog increase can worsen cash or hide low-margin contracts.

For aerospace, inspect deliveries, certified capacity, engines/maintenance and supplier constraints. For defense, distinguish funded awards from maximum contract ceilings and unexercised options. For electrification/automation, confirm the customer's funded buildout and actual ship schedule. Orders are not automatically today's revenue.

Query `"issuer" orders funded backlog delivery cash guidance` and `"customer or program" delay cancellation cost overrun`. Compare similar end-market peers and customer disclosures against vendor claims. Countercheck strike/safety issues, certification, warranty charges, inventory and fixed-price losses.

Watch named programs/customers and deliveries, not a generic industrial boom. Sources: [Census M3 orders/shipments](https://www.census.gov/manufacturing/m3/), [Fed production](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/current/), plus original issuer/customer contract disclosures.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
