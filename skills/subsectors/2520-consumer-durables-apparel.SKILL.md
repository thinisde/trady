# Consumer Durables & Apparel subsector

GICS industry group 2520; parent [sector guide](../sectors/25-consumer-discretionary.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose homebuilding, household durables/leisure products or apparel/luxury. For homebuilders, inspect orders, cancellations, backlog conversion, incentives, mortgage buy-downs, land obligations and gross margin. For durables, check retailer/channel inventory and replacement demand. For apparel/luxury, compare full-price sales, geographic/customer mix, markdowns and inventory age.

Read actual issuer orders/results alongside housing and spending context. Housing starts, permits, sales, closes and recognized builder revenue have different clocks. A nominal sales gain can coexist with lower units or deteriorating mix.

Query `"issuer" orders cancellation incentive inventory margin` or `"brand" full price markdown wholesale demand`, then financing/supplier/counterfeit or weakness queries relevant to the branch. Countercheck excess stock, returns, credit affordability, fashion risk and channel stuffing. A new store or celebrity endorsement alone does not prove incremental earnings.

Watch branch-specific demand and inventory events, plus financing/inputs where material. Sources: [Census housing/retail releases](https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/), [BEA consumption](https://www.bea.gov/data/consumer-spending).

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
