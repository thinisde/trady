# Equity Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) subsector

GICS industry group 6010; parent [sector guide](../sectors/60-real-estate.SKILL.md). Apply [the daily funnel](../../docs/v1/sector-workflow.md) and source comparison. Use actual current members; nonmember counterparties remain research entities.

Choose the actual property branch: data center, industrial/logistics, residential, office, retail, specialized/health-related or diversified. Compare same-store net operating income, occupancy, leasing spreads, lease duration/escalators, tenant credit/concentration, development commitments, debt maturities and interest coverage. A data-center lease pipeline is not occupied revenue without power/capacity and commencement terms.

Reconcile FFO to GAAP and inspect issuer-specific AFFO adjustments; neither is automatically distributable cash. Separate property sale gains, same-store growth and acquisitions. Match property/geography/lease structure when comparing peers.

Query `"issuer" same store NOI occupancy leasing spread FFO debt maturity` and `"tenant or project" default concession power delay`. Countercheck tenant failures, capex/concessions, refinancing/dilution, falling property values and unfunded development. High dividend yield can accompany deteriorating economics.

Watch material tenants, lease commencements, financing and property-type demand. Sources: [Nareit FFO guidance](https://www.reit.com/nareit/advocacy/policy/nareit-ffo-white-paper-and-related-implementation), original issuer/tenant filings.

Store the chosen operating branch, KPI definitions/periods, peer comparisons, primary/counterevidence and exclusions in `SCREENING`; carry material drivers into the company profile/watchdog. Missing material facts mean blocked research, never improvised certainty.
